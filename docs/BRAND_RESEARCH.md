# RetailPulse Korea — Brand & Competitive Naming Research

**Research date:** 2026-08-26 KST  
**Status:** research snapshot only; no final rename approved.

## 1. Current brand

Current product/code/UI name remains:

`RetailPulse Korea`

Meaning:

- Retail = physical retail / shopping / stores / commercial demand
- Pulse = the current pulse or changing condition of the market
- Korea = national scope / future expansion beyond Seoul

Strength: meaning matches the product well.  
Weakness: `RetailPulse` is already used by multiple unrelated/adjacent retail products and services, reducing distinctiveness and SEO ownership.

## 2. Candidate: Korea Retail Signal

Meaning:

`Korea Retail Signal` = a service that turns multiple real-world Korean retail/tourism/visitor indicators into actionable signals.

Why it fits:

- `Signal` matches the product's underlying concept better than `Pulse` because the product combines airport, tourism, weather, population and area data into leading decision signals.
- `Korea` keeps national expansion open even though the MVP is Seoul-first.
- `Retail` keeps the B2B/store/shop-demand meaning visible.

## 3. Exact-name search result

Broad public-web searches on 2026-08-26 did **not** surface an obvious established product using the exact full phrase:

`Korea Retail Signal`

This is positive but is not proof of legal availability or domain ownership.

Search-engine absence must not be treated as trademark clearance.

## 4. Important collision: Retail Signal

The core phrase `Retail Signal` is already in active use in adjacent markets.

### Retail Signal — NYC

Website: `https://www.retailsignal.co/`

Positioning observed:

- NYC retail intelligence
- site selection strategy
- lease advisory
- corridor analysis
- foot-traffic data
- market intelligence for brands expanding into New York retail locations

This is not the same product as RPK, but the name and retail-intelligence category are close enough to create brand/SEO/confusion risk.

### Propheus — Retail Signal

Observed through Propheus/LinkedIn public material.

Positioning observed:

- weekly store intelligence
- 14-day outlook
- local events
- competitor promotions
- weather
- demand signals
- inventory/staffing/promotion recommendations

This is functionally closer to RetailPulse Korea because it also converts location/event/weather context into forward-looking store decisions.

Therefore `Retail Signal` must not be treated as an uncontested core brand phrase.

## 5. Why this matters

Even if `Korea Retail Signal` is technically different from `Retail Signal`, the name has four concerns:

1. SEO — exact searches may still mix with established `Retail Signal` services.
2. Distinctiveness — `Retail` + `Signal` are both descriptive industry words.
3. Trademark strength — highly descriptive names can be harder to protect strongly.
4. Global expansion — an eventual non-Korea/global product could collide more directly with existing Retail Signal businesses.

The `Korea` prefix lowers confusion somewhat but does not remove the underlying issue.

## 7. Current naming recommendation

Do **not** rename yet during Cloudflare production work.

Current shortlist direction:

### A. Korea Retail Signal

Keep as a strong descriptive candidate because the product meaning is excellent.

But downgrade it from “clear winner” to “strong candidate requiring legal/SEO/domain review” because `Retail Signal` is already used by active adjacent businesses.

Potentially safer from direct name collision while still matching foreign visitors and shopping demand.

Worth exploring before final domain purchase if the owner wants stronger long-term trademark/SEO ownership.

## 8. Competitive product landscape

The product does not operate in an empty market.

### Public/institutional substitutes in Korea

#### Seoul Commercial District Analysis / Seoul Open Data

Provides commercial-district population and other area-analysis data. Seoul living-population datasets include domestic and foreign population estimates, and the city has shifted several population products toward 250m-grid data in 2026.

Strength vs RPK:

- authoritative local datasets
- deep historical/local population detail

RPK target differentiation:

- combine multiple sources
- simplify into today/tomorrow/best-time decisions
- foreign visitor + shopping focus
- prospective forecast/outcome scoring

#### Korea Tourism Data Lab

Provides regional tourism analysis including foreign visitor status, telecom-based visitor metrics, tourism consumption and other regional indicators.

Strength vs RPK:

- strong official tourism data breadth
- visitor and tourism-consumption context

RPK target differentiation:

- operational retail decision layer rather than raw tourism dashboard
- area + airport + weather + forecast integration
- multilingual visitor/business UI

### Global / adjacent commercial competitors

#### Retail Signal — NYC

Retail site-selection and market-intelligence advisory.

#### Propheus Retail Signal

Forward-looking store intelligence using events, competitor activity, weather and demand signals.

#### Placer.ai / location-intelligence category

Foot traffic, trade-area and location analytics are established commercial categories. RPK should not claim novelty merely for showing foot traffic or area demand.

## 9. Product differentiation target

RPK should aim to win on the combination rather than on owning a single raw dataset:

- Seoul-first, foreign-visitor/shopping specific
- today / tomorrow / best-time orientation
- Incheon Airport + tourism + weather + area signals
- clear official-history vs proxy vs forecast vs actual labels
- multilingual tourist + professional use
- forecast archive with later outcome scoring
- useful actions rather than raw charts only

This remains a hypothesis until validated with real users and measured forecasting performance.

## 10. Before final rename — mandatory checks

Before buying a final domain or changing the repository/product name, perform a dedicated clearance pass:

1. Korean trademark search through KIPRIS / KIPO interfaces
2. international trademark search where relevant
3. exact and confusingly-similar English/Korean brand searches
4. `.com` and relevant country-domain availability
5. major social handle availability
6. GitHub / app-store / SaaS-name collisions
7. SEO query competition
8. pronunciation and Korean/English/Chinese/Japanese readability
9. visual-logo fit
10. future expansion beyond Seoul/Korea

Search-engine checks on 2026-08-26 did not return indexed KIPRIS/KIPO results for the exact phrases, but this **does not count as an official trademark clearance** because official database interfaces must be checked directly.
