# KORETAIL — Brand & Competitive Naming Research

**Research date:** 2026-08-26 KST  
**Status:** historical naming research; final brand now approved as `KORETAIL`.

## 1. Final brand decision

The owner approved:

`KORETAIL`

Meaning:

`Korea + Retail`

Preferred descriptor:

`Retail Demand Signals for Korea`

Canonical decision file:

- `docs/BRAND_DECISION_KORETAIL.md`

The previous public name `RetailPulse Korea` is now legacy branding and should be migrated out of public-facing UI/SEO/marketing copy when safe.

Technical identifiers may temporarily retain `retailpulse-korea` for compatibility with GitHub, Cloudflare, D1, environment variables and deployment configuration.

## 2. Why RetailPulse Korea was not kept

`RetailPulse Korea` matched the product meaning well:

- Retail = physical retail / shopping / stores / commercial demand
- Pulse = the changing condition of the market
- Korea = national scope / future expansion beyond Seoul

However, `RetailPulse` is already used by multiple unrelated/adjacent retail products and services, reducing distinctiveness and SEO ownership.

## 3. Why Korea Retail Signal was not selected

`Korea Retail Signal` matched the product concept because the service turns airport, tourism, weather, visitor and area data into forward-looking retail-demand signals.

However, the core phrase `Retail Signal` is already in active use in adjacent markets.

### Retail Signal — NYC

Observed positioning:

- NYC retail intelligence
- site selection strategy
- lease advisory
- corridor analysis
- foot-traffic data
- market intelligence for expanding brands

### Propheus — Retail Signal

Observed positioning:

- weekly store intelligence
- 14-day outlook
- local events
- competitor promotions
- weather
- demand signals
- inventory/staffing/promotion recommendations

This is close enough to KORETAIL's intended category that `Retail Signal` should not be treated as an uncontested core brand phrase.

## 4. Why KORETAIL was selected

`KORETAIL` is a coined brand built from Korea + Retail.

Advantages:

- more distinctive than `RetailPulse` or `Retail Signal`
- keeps the retail/B2B meaning
- does not lock the product to Seoul
- can support national expansion
- short enough to function as a real product/company brand
- works with a descriptive subtitle for immediate clarity

Preferred presentation:

```text
KORETAIL
Retail Demand Signals for Korea
```

## 5. Competitive product landscape

The product does not operate in an empty market.

### Public/institutional substitutes in Korea

#### Seoul Commercial District Analysis / Seoul Open Data

Provides commercial-district population and other area-analysis data. Seoul living-population datasets include domestic and foreign population estimates.

Strength vs KORETAIL:

- authoritative local datasets
- deep historical/local population detail

KORETAIL target differentiation:

- combine multiple sources
- simplify into today/tomorrow/best-time decisions
- foreign visitor + shopping focus
- prospective forecast/outcome scoring

#### Korea Tourism Data Lab

Provides regional tourism analysis including foreign visitor status, telecom-based visitor metrics, tourism consumption and other regional indicators.

Strength vs KORETAIL:

- strong official tourism-data breadth
- visitor and tourism-consumption context

KORETAIL target differentiation:

- operational retail-decision layer rather than raw tourism dashboard
- area + airport + weather + forecast integration
- multilingual visitor/business UI

### Global / adjacent commercial competitors

#### Retail Signal — NYC

Retail site-selection and market-intelligence advisory.

#### Propheus Retail Signal

Forward-looking store intelligence using events, competitor activity, weather and demand signals.

#### Placer.ai / location-intelligence category

Foot traffic, trade-area and location analytics are established commercial categories. KORETAIL should not claim novelty merely for showing foot traffic or area demand.

## 6. Product differentiation target

KORETAIL should aim to win on the combination rather than on owning a single raw dataset:

- Seoul-first, foreign-visitor/shopping specific
- today / tomorrow / best-time orientation
- Incheon Airport + tourism + weather + area signals
- clear official-history vs proxy vs forecast vs actual labels
- multilingual tourist + professional use
- forecast archive with later outcome scoring
- useful actions rather than raw charts only

This remains a hypothesis until validated with real users and measured forecasting performance.

## 7. Remaining legal/domain clearance

Selecting `KORETAIL` as the product brand is a product decision, not legal trademark clearance.

Before buying or permanently cutting over a final domain, perform a dedicated clearance pass:

1. Korean trademark search through KIPRIS / KIPO interfaces
2. international trademark search where relevant
3. exact and confusingly similar English/Korean brand searches
4. `.com` and relevant country-domain availability
5. major social handle availability
6. GitHub / app-store / SaaS-name collisions
7. SEO query competition
8. pronunciation and Korean/English/Chinese/Japanese readability
9. visual-logo fit
10. future expansion beyond Seoul/Korea

Search-engine absence must not be treated as trademark clearance.

## 8. Migration rule

Public-facing brand migration to `KORETAIL` is approved now.

Technical-resource migration is separate and must remain compatibility-safe.

Therefore:

- migrate UI/SEO/marketing copy to `KORETAIL`
- use `Retail Demand Signals for Korea` as the preferred descriptor
- do not reintroduce `Korea Retail Signal` as the final brand
- preserve active legacy infrastructure identifiers until a safe migration plan exists
- do not break Cloudflare/D1/GitHub Actions/DNS merely for cosmetic consistency
