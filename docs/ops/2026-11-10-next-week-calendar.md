# Next-week calendar correction, 2026-11-10

## Reproduction and cause

The reported Korean home brief used the reference date 2026-11-10 and correctly
calculated next week as November 16–22. At base
`2273f4f8da4bb8bce0df48bbb36e07171dc2ee71`, neither the full reviewed holiday
seed, its small summary, nor the calendar-context seed contained any overlapping
entry. `planningHolidays` consequently returned an empty list. The UI described
that completed empty result as “확인된 대표 일정을 대조하고 있습니다.”, implying
an outstanding request that was not actually occurring.

This was a data omission plus misleading empty-state copy, not a failed
passenger-data lookup or a Monday/Sunday calculation error. The seed is
explicitly representative and incomplete; an empty week cannot prove that every
country has no holidays.

## Reviewed additions

- Mexico: November 16, 2026 only, Revolution Day mandatory rest day under labour
  law. The [PROFEDET 2026 list](https://www.gob.mx/profedet/articulos/sabes-cuales-son-los-dias-de-descanso-obligatorio-para-este-2026?idiom=es)
  states the third Monday of November. Its indexed official text was available,
  but direct retrieval returned HTTP 403; this is not recorded as a successful
  direct fetch. The exact November 16 date was cross-checked against the
  [Labour Ministry's 2026 official-gazette calendar](https://sidof.segob.gob.mx/notas/docFuente/5776858),
  which was directly readable. November 20 and nearby weekends are not added.
- Czech Republic: November 17, 2026 only, Freedom and Democracy Day. Directly
  checked in the [Foreign Ministry's 2026 list](https://mzv.gov.cz/london/en/visa_and_consular_information/czech_bank_holidays_2020/index.html)
  and [Czech National Bank's 2026 list](https://www.cnb.cz/en/public/media-service/schedules-and-other-info/bank-holidays-in-the-czech-republic/).

The new records carry their own November 10 review date. Previous review dates,
the original snapshot header and all existing entries remain unchanged. The
small summary is regenerated by `node scripts/sync-airport-calendar-summary.mjs`.
Record/method/jurisdiction counts reflect the two additions without claiming
complete annual or all-country coverage.

## Implementation and regression contract

- The UI uses the same explicit KST civil reference date and next Monday–Sunday
  window. It does not extend the window just to find something to display.
- Empty results now state that no representative dates are registered for that
  period and that this is not proof of no holidays worldwide. Korean, English,
  Chinese and Japanese empty states are provided.
- No provider API, recurring job, calendar collector, D1 write, access setting,
  theme, typography scale or existing event detail is changed.
- Unit coverage includes the reported week without runtime/passenger data,
  later evidence exclusion, inclusive overlaps, Sunday/month/year/leap
  boundaries, empty data, and expired/conditional/unverified exclusions.
- Seven browser cases cover 320/390/1280px, failed optional APIs, native detail
  controls, a genuinely empty future week, and a historical selection with a
  different device timezone and clock from the KST reference date. Two cases also
  verify successful empty optional-calendar responses, with and without an
  unrelated replacement scope, after an existing source-state UI proves the
  response was consumed.
- The required `혁` and `쟁` glyphs are added to the existing font with all prior
  shapes/widths preserved at weights 100–900. See
  `docs/licenses/FONT-ASSET-SOURCES.md` and the narrow glyph record for exact
  hashes and invariants. Protection still checks all original paths; only the
  exact font bytes and cache-key substitution are updated.
