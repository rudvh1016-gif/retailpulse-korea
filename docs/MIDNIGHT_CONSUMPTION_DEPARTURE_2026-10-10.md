## Midnight and incomplete publication

- The existing complete A1 scan already retains every explicitly dated future departure published in the same population. No separate next-day provider request or fixed six-day assumption is added.
- The summary and flight API fresh plus stale cache windows expire by KST midnight. Default-summary and flight client URLs carry a calendar cache key; that key does not override the server's actual service day. A fixed tomorrow flight selection also refreshes at midnight, independently of its normal two-minute freshness interval. Existing date race guards, focus coalescing and server-clock navigation remain.
- The existing early A1 workflow moves from 04:07 to 00:07 KST, preserving its three classified-transient attempts and existing scheduler ownership. A page retries once after a 20-second backoff. No recurring workflow or Worker Cron is added. GitHub scheduling can be delayed; 00:07 collection is not a promise of immediate midnight actual-operation data. Held, clearly labelled official schedules supply the immediate display continuity.
- Production A1 entry points reserve at most 125 request attempts per job against an atomic shared 500-attempt rolling 24-hour ledger with an extra 30-minute duration margin. Normal completed scans settle to the exact attempts counted before each request. A crashed job or failed settlement retains the full reservation. Unknown/exhausted budget permits zero provider requests. The legacy one-page manual entry point reserves its one-call ceiling too. Existing tables are reused; no migration, new key or paid service.
- A successful, complete external population without today's rows is `NOT_YET_PUBLISHED`, separate from transport/schema failure. It does not increment the failure counter or advance the observed clock. Good observed rows and clocks remain; valid future schedules are independently retained. Absence does not establish zero flights or withdrawal. Missing future dates and missing terminal coverage preserve held snapshots and their original clocks; explicit cancelled rows remain excluded.
- T1, T2 and concourse can independently select their held schedule when current departure records for that building are unavailable. Good records in another building remain. The whole-airport list keeps its collected basis and names available building fallbacks, rather than mixing schedules into operational totals. Expected passengers, observed queues and counted flights retain separate definitions.
- A1 exposes a complete all-terminal population. Independently filtered T2 provider retry parameters have not been verified, so no T2-only API request is invented. Retries address classified A1 transport failures, not an assumption that external nonpublication is a recoverable error.

Published zero survives. Missing, past and future intervals do not manufacture a current observation; forecasts are not observed waiting people.
