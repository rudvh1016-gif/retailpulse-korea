# Japan, Vietnam and Thailand — concise declaration guidance

Official pages were directly reviewed on **2026-10-22** for the limited changes below. Existing monetary/quantity limits and country review dates are unchanged. This review is not an assertion that every special-case rule or later amendment has been exhaustively checked.

## Japan

- [Japan Customs exemption scope](https://www.customs.go.jp/kaigairyoko/menzei.htm): under-20 travelers have no alcohol/tobacco exemption; under-6 travelers retain their own-use-only branch. The adult bottle-size sentence is not shown for ages 6–19, while accompanied/unaccompanied baggage aggregation remains. Adult wording and the numeric publication snapshot are unchanged.
- [Passenger declaration procedure](https://www.customs.go.jp/english/summary/passenger.htm): all arriving passengers declare belongings, including within the allowance. Dutiable/restricted goods require the red channel. The action does not promise admission of prohibited/restricted goods on payment.
- The general limit's original effective date was not established by this scoped review. The existing single-item/set treatment is retained without a tax calculation.

## Vietnam

- [Decree 167/2025, Article 1(29)(a), replacing Article 59(2)](https://congbaocdn.chinhphu.vn/CongBaoCP/VanBan/2025/6/45351/57292-1-2025883-884167-2025-nd-cp.pdf): excess accompanied baggage must be declared before passing customs inspection. The provision appears on PDF page 44 (printed page 45); Article 4 on PDF page 85 states the **2025-08-15** effective date.
- This declaration action moves into the visible core. Existing personal-item separation, minor alcohol/tobacco exclusion, alternative quantities and listed-occupation exception remain unchanged.
- [Government notice on electronic/heated tobacco](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/cam-san-xuat-kinh-doanh-nhap-khau-chua-chap-van-chuyen-su-dung-thuoc-la-dien-tu-thuoc-la-nung-nong-tu-nam-2025-119241214164955394.htm), published 2024-12-17, was re-read; the existing 2025 import-prohibition warning stays visible.
- The decree also contains temporary luggage-storage provisions. Those are **not added to this UI**: airport-specific acceptance, goods eligibility, fees, collection procedure and cross-airport availability need separate verification. No Chinese custody procedure is applied to Vietnam.

## Thailand

- [Thai Customs airport passenger guidance](https://www.customs.go.th/list_strc_simple_neted.php?ini_content=individual_160503_03_160905_01&lang=en&left_menu=menu_individual_submenu_01_160421_01): ordinary goods requiring declaration go to the declaration channel with a passport and invoice/receipt if available. Excess alcohol/tobacco is directed to designated customs boxes, not presented as automatically importable after paying tax.
- Own-use/reasonable-quantity scope and food/prohibited/restricted-goods distinctions remain. The unresolved alcohol/tobacco age rule is shown beside those product figures, not only in a separate disclosure. The gift/special-purpose branch is unchanged.
- The general guidance's original effective date remains unverified. No fee, storage or re-export entitlement is inferred.

## Presentation boundary

The reviewed ordinary results show their existing limits, then a short action and warning, followed by collapsed product/source detail. Incomplete, unknown, special-purpose and child exceptions still use their own branches. The section remains Korean (`lang="ko"`); locale-route coverage is not a claim of translated legal guidance. Automatic-access configuration, approved source baselines and database records are unchanged.
