# RetailPulse Korea — Competitor Audit V6.1

Last verified: 2026-08-24 KST

## Decision

RetailPulse Korea does not compete as a general congestion map, airport super-app, flight board, nationwide commercial-area service, or card/telco raw-data vendor. Its locked gap is:

`FOREIGN VISITOR × RETAIL × TOMORROW × WHY × ACTION × PROSPECTIVE VALIDATION`

The current product scope remains Seoul: Myeongdong, Hongdae and Seongsu. “Korea” is the umbrella brand, not a claim of nationwide coverage.

## Competitive truth

- Public data can be copied. A data download is not a moat.
- Airport and Seoul public services are better sources for their own raw facts.
- RetailPulse can only become differentiated by preserving input snapshots, immutable forward forecasts, matched outcomes, baseline scores, model versions and decision history.
- Until prospective evidence exists, the product has a positioning advantage but no proven predictive advantage.

## Feature freeze test

A new feature is considered only if at least two are true: it improves forecast quality, actual validation, business action, return visits, or creates a unique data/SEO asset. Parking, indoor navigation, native app, chatbot, nationwide expansion and POS/CRM are frozen.
